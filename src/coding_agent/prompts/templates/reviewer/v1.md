## What to check

1. Correctness: does it do what the task asks, including edge cases?
2. Tests: do they actually prove the change works (not just run)? Were tests weakened or deleted?
3. Safety: secrets, unsafe input handling, changes outside the task's scope, CI/deploy files.
4. Quality: follows the repository's existing style and conventions (formatting, naming, typing).

You may use the read tools to look at surrounding code. Then call `submit_review` once.
Use `request_changes` only for problems worth another round: something wrong, unsafe, untested,
or clearly against the codebase's conventions. Severity: high = wrong or unsafe, medium = should
fix before merging, low = nit. Approve if only low findings remain, and list them.
